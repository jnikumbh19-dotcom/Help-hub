BR-001
Only admins can approve/reject service providers, or verify them.

BR-002
Business owners can only update their own service provider profiles.

BR-003
Only admins can manage city zones.

BR-004
Users can only manage their own profiles and ICE details.

BR-005
Users can file complaints against any service provider. Only admins can update the status of complaints.

BR-006
Audit logs must be created whenever an admin or business owner performs a critical action (verification, activation, rejection, update).
